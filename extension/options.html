Tasks repo

The extension keeps its own copy of your tasks and syncs it with your tasks repo on GitHub, like the desktop and Android apps. Use a separate fine-grained personal access token scoped to only the tasks repo, with Contents: Read and write, so you can revoke it on its own.

The token is stored in Chrome's extension storage on this computer. Unlike the desktop app's token, it is not encrypted by the operating system, so anyone with access to your Chrome profile could read it.

Sync

Changes go up a couple of seconds after you make them. New changes from your other devices come down when you open the side panel and every 15 minutes.
